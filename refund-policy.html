Refunds & Cancellations Policy

Last Updated: 3 November 2026

At BookNest, we want customers to have a clear understanding of our refund and cancellation process before purchasing digital books.

1. Digital Products

BookNest offers digital books. Since digital products may become accessible immediately after successful payment, all purchases are generally considered final once the digital content has been delivered.

2. Eligible Refund Requests

Customers may contact us to request a review if:

  • Payment was deducted more than once for the same order.
  • Payment was successful but the purchased book was not made available.
  • The transaction amount was incorrect due to a verified technical error.

Each request will be reviewed individually. A refund will be issued where the request is found to be valid under applicable law and our policies.

3. Cancellation Policy

Customers should contact us as soon as possible if they wish to cancel an order. Once a digital book has been delivered or accessed, cancellation may not be possible, subject to applicable consumer rights.

4. How to Request a Refund

Contact our support team with the following information:

  • Customer name
  • Order ID or payment reference
  • Payment date and amount
  • Reason for the refund request

Submit your request through our Contact Us page.

5. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be processed through the original payment method wherever possible. The time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider and bank.

6. Contact Us

For questions or assistance regarding refunds and cancellations, please visit our Contact Us page.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 3 November 2026

BookNest respects the privacy of its customers. This policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you visit or purchase products through our website.

1. Information We Collect

During checkout, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number and order details required to process your purchase.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and verify customer orders.
  • To provide access to purchased digital products.
  • To respond to customer questions and support requests.
  • To maintain website security and prevent misuse.

3. Payment Information

Payments are processed through Cashfree. Payment information is handled by the payment provider according to its applicable privacy and security practices. BookNest does not intend to store customers' card PINs, passwords or full card security details.

4. Sharing of Information

Customer information may be shared with service providers when necessary to process payments, provide website services or comply with legal obligations.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect customer information. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies and Website Data

Our website may use browser storage or similar technologies to support shopping cart functionality and improve the customer experience.

7. Your Privacy Requests

You may contact us to ask about your personal information or request assistance with privacy-related concerns, subject to applicable law.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, please visit our Contact Us page.

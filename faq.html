Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?
To place an order, browse our catalog, add desired books to your cart, then proceed to the cart page and click "Buy Now". Follow the checkout instructions and complete payment via Razorpay.
Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept payments through Razorpay, including debit cards, credit cards, net banking, UPI, and popular wallets.
What is your refund policy?
If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you may request a refund within 7 days by contacting our support at support@booknest.com. Please refer to our Refund Policy page for detailed information.
How do I track my order?
Currently, order tracking is not automated. After payment confirmation, you will receive an email.
Who can I contact for support?
You can reach us at support@booknest.com or call us at +91-7893022550. Visit our Contact page for more info.
